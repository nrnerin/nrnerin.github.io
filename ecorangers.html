Eco-Rangers AR 🌱

Interactive Augmented Reality Educational Application

About the Project

Eco-Rangers AR is an educational augmented reality application designed to create interactive and engaging learning experiences for children through multimedia and AR technology. Traditional learning methods can sometimes feel less engaging for children, making it difficult to maintain attention and interest during educational activities. Eco-Rangers AR combines multimedia content with augmented reality features to create a more interactive learning environment where children can explore educational content visually and actively.

Features

  • Interactive AR Experience
  • Educational Multimedia Content
  • 3D Object Visualization
  • Eco-Mission Games
  • Interactive Quiz
  • Avatar Evolution
  • User-Friendly Interface
  • Child-Friendly Learning Design

Technologies Used

Unity Figma AR Development UI/UX Multimedia Design
