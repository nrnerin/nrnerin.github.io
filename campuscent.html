Campus Cent 💸

Student Budget Management Application

About the Project

Campus Cent is a budgeting application designed to help university students manage their expenses, savings, and spending habits more effectively. Many university students struggle with financial management and often overspend due to lack of budgeting awareness and tracking tools. Campus Cent provides an easy-to-use budgeting platform that allows students to record expenses, monitor spending categories, and plan monthly budgets in a more organized way.

Features

  • Expense Tracking
  • Budget Planning
  • Student Discount
  • Money History
  • Student Card Display
  • Bill Reminder
  • Financial Health Dashboard
  • Simple Student-Friendly UI

Technologies Used

Canva Firebase Android Studio UI/UX Mobile Design
