Campus Cent 💸

Student Budget Management Application

About the Project

Campus Cent is a budgeting application designed to help university students track their expenses and manage their spending habits more efficiently.

Problem

Many students struggle to monitor their daily expenses and savings, leading to poor financial management.

Solution

Campus Cent provides an easy-to-use interface for students to record expenses, categorize spending, and manage personal budgets.

Technologies Used

Figma Firebase Android Studio UI/UX
